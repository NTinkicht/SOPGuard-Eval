## Deliverables requested now

- **Initial reply (suggested within 7–10 working days):** sections A and B as far as known, plus the person responsible for C/D.
- **Next step (only after local authorization):** a completed **aggregate-only** worksheet using [blank template](lab_aggregate_feasibility_template.csv), ideally within a timeline proposed by the laboratory.
- **Later and separately:** ethics/waiver decision, data agreement, SOP use permissions and documented secure processing route before accessing any patient-level record.

**Explicitly NOT requested:** patient names, accession identifiers, individual CBC results, identifiable case lists, real screenshots, patient-level CSVs, local proprietary SOPs, or research participant adjudication at this stage.

This questionnaire is for scientific feasibility **only**, not a clinical procedure or institutional research authorization.
