# Partner laboratory and ethics readiness - working checklist

This document is for collaboration negotiation only; no partner or permission has been secured by the repository.

## Minimum partner capabilities

- Accredited clinical laboratory, named haematology lead and current analyser(s) plus software version.
- Access to approved local SOPs, applicable IFUs and validation documents under explicit terms, including publication and derivative-rule restrictions.
- Feasibility count of original CBC flags/investigations and follow-up specimens or corrected measurements.
- At least two to three qualified independent adjudicators and a mechanism to avoid rule-author conflict of interest.
- Institutional ethics/IRB decision (approval or formal waiver), documented privacy review and data-use agreement.
- Secure research environment and a specific approved list of model providers/processing jurisdictions.
- Documented export capabilities and expected missingness for optical/research parameters, flags and specimen notes.

## Pre-extraction study

Count plausible eligible **pairs** by interference category, analyser/software version and availability of original/repeat measurements. Flag scarcity; do not claim a published benchmark size before this count.

## Agreements must state

Roles as controller/processor, minimization, security, retention/destruction, sharing/publication rights, cloud restrictions, incident handling, cross-border processing, auditability, consent/waiver basis and prohibited uses. Obtain legal/privacy review under applicable local health data regulation.

## Public artifacts versus restricted assets

Public: protocol, software after research approval, schemas, synthetic worked examples, bibliographic evidence, aggregated statistics and suitably reviewed rule abstractions.

Restricted: patient episode data, private expert notes, local SOP text if controlled, licensed IFU excerpts, linkage maps, exact timestamps, credentials and signed agreements.

## Go/no-go

**NO-GO for case-level research or scoring** without clinical collaborator, governance clearance, SOP applicability and independent annotation plan. This is not a blocker for writing public, nonclinical research documents.
