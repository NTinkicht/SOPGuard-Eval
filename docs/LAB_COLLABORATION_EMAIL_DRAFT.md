**Subject:** SOPGuard-Eval | Request for laboratory research contributions and feasibility information

Dear Colleagues,

Thank you for your willingness to collaborate on SOPGuard-Eval.

We have now refined the scientific proposal into a focused, retrospective laboratory-medicine study. Our objective is to assess whether large language models can incorrectly interpret analytically unreliable complete blood count (CBC) results as genuine disease or suggest unsafe laboratory actions, and whether providing the applicable laboratory SOP reduces these risks.

Rather than scheduling an introductory meeting, I would like to move directly to the practical preparation of the study. I am attaching a brief project summary and a laboratory response checklist describing exactly what we need from each participating laboratory.

**Could your team please help us with the following initial tasks?**

1. Nominate a haematology laboratory specialist who could act as clinical co-investigator, along with a contact for research governance.
2. Identify your CBC analyser family and the types of analytical interference routinely investigated.
3. Confirm whether original flagged results, corrective investigations, repeat measurements, final reporting decisions, and suitable clinical control records are retained, as well as the approximate historical period available.
4. Indicate whether the institution would authorize an **aggregate-only feasibility count** of eligible cases, and who could prepare it after the necessary local permissions.
5. Identify the applicable ethics, privacy, SOP-use, and AI/data-processing approval process.

An initial written response within **7–10 working days**, even if some questions remain pending, would allow us to finalize the feasible research scope and identify next steps promptly. If authorizations for aggregate counts take longer, please simply indicate the appropriate approval route and anticipated timeframe.

**We are not requesting patient-level data, individual laboratory reports, or confidential SOP copies at this stage.** Any subsequent data access or use of LLMs would require the institution's formal permissions, independent clinical review, and approved processing arrangements.

Once we receive the initial feasibility information, I will integrate your laboratory's input into the protocol and coordinate the next steps for scientific collaboration, ethics and data governance.

Thank you again for your collaboration. I look forward to moving the proposal into its feasibility phase.
