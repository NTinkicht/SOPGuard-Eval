# Four-model exploratory review synthesis

**Provenance:** Gemini, Grok, Mistral Vibe and Claude gave independent *AI-generated* reviews in the planning conversation (October 2026). They are not credentialed panels, peer reviewers or sources of clinical authority.

## Convergence

All recommend discarding the initial multi-vendor LLM clinical application as the first research study. They recommend an evaluation-focused study with a deterministic baseline, explicit SOP versioning, clinical oversight, numerical validity, and measured abstention.

## Distinct contributions

- Gemini: benchmark-first focus and strong skepticism of LLM necessity.
- Grok: modest scope (one analyser, initial interference classes), rule-based strong comparator and clinical partner requirement.
- Mistral: human arm, provenance taxonomy, SOP wording generalization and risk/coverage.
- Claude: empirical paired outcomes; GFHC 2025 prior-art emphasis; independent adjudication rather than circular rule-generated truth; oracle-SOP arm; genuine-pathology controls; pilot-driven power; separation of clinical and research software.

## Adjustments accepted by this project

A deterministic baseline is *not* assumed infallible. Post-intervention runs are supportive but may remain interfered and cannot always serve as definitive truth. Repeated model calls are clustered observations. Sample size and effect thresholds remain undecided until a feasibility pilot. 'Measured'/'derived' labels refer to site-validated methods and specific quantities, never blanket manufacturer capabilities.

## Research identity

A standalone laboratory-medicine safety benchmark with limited methodological reuse from evaluation, FHIR/provenance and adversarial robustness projects. The study must not become a duplicate RAG poisoning benchmark.

## Limitations

AI reviews may contain inaccuracies and invented or overconfident evidence. Verify each externally stated clinical and regulatory claim against primary publications, IFUs and local experts. Do not imply a lab collaborator, protocol registration, ethics waiver or implementation exists.
